Wall Balls decide your HYROX

The fastest 10% and the slowest 10% of 844 finishers are 64:48 apart. Here is exactly where that time goes.

09 September 2026 · Splitline

Every HYROX athlete has a theory about where their race is lost. The splits settle it. Across 844 finishers, the gap between the fastest tenth of the field and the slowest is 64:48 — and it is not spread evenly.

The share of the total time difference attributable to each segment. The eight runs are pooled.

Wall Balls alone accounts for 15.1% of the entire gap: 4:47 for the fast group against 14:34 for the slow one. That is a single station taking almost three times as long.

Take the three worst stations together and they explain 31.2% of the difference between a fast race and a slow one. The ergs — the two machines everyone trains most — barely feature.

The roxzone nobody measures

Transition time is 14.2% of the gap on its own: 7:34 for the fast group, 16:45 for the slow. It is the only segment of a HYROX race that requires no fitness to improve, and almost nobody trains it.
